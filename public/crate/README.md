# Starter crate

Audio that ships inside the app so it makes sound the first time it opens: a few short tracks
for the decks and one-shots and loops for the sound pads. Filled in Milestone 1.

## Rules

- **Only audio we may redistribute.** CC0 / public domain, or recordings made for this project.
  No "royalty-free" packs whose licence forbids redistribution, and nothing from a streaming
  service or a store purchase.
- Every file is listed in `crate.json` with its title, author, source link and licence.
- Keep it small (target: under 15 MB in total). The whole crate is cached for offline use.
- Tracks are chosen to mix well with each other: steady tempo, compatible keys.
